Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Creva Solutions and our services.

What IT services does Creva Solutions provide?

Creva Solutions provides web development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, software development, digital marketing and branding.

Does Creva Solutions provide web development?

Yes. Creva Solutions builds business websites, e-commerce stores and custom web applications, and provides maintenance and support after launch. See our web development page.

What does Creva Solutions offer for businesses?

For businesses we offer IT services such as websites, apps, custom software, digital marketing and branding, and civil services such as 2D drafting, 3D modeling, planning and civil design for property and construction projects.

What is UI/UX design?

UI/UX design is the practice of designing how a digital product looks (UI, the user interface) and how it works for users (UX, the user experience). Read more on our UI/UX design page.

What civil services does Creva Solutions provide?

Creva Solutions provides 2D drafting, 3D modeling, planning and architectural services and civil design services for residential and small commercial projects.

What is 2D drafting?

2D drafting is the creation of flat, dimensioned technical drawings such as floor plans, elevations and sections that describe how a building is arranged and built. See our 2D drafting page.

What is 3D modeling?

3D modeling creates a digital three-dimensional model of a building so you can view its shape, materials and lighting before construction. See our 3D modeling page.

Where is Creva Solutions based and who do you work with?

Creva Solutions is based in Sankarapuram, Kallakurichi district, Tamil Nadu, India. We work with local businesses and homeowners in the region and with clients elsewhere in India remotely.
