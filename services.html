What is 2D drafting?

2D drafting is the creation of flat, dimensioned technical drawings such as floor plans, elevations and sections that describe how a building is arranged and built. See our 2D drafting page.

What is 3D modeling?

3D modeling creates a digital three-dimensional model of a building so you can view its shape, materials and lighting before construction. See our 3D modeling page.
