About the service

Design That Makes Your Product Easy to Use

UI/UX design is how a digital product looks and how it works for the person using it. UX (user experience) is the journey — can people find what they need and finish what they came to do? UI (user interface) is the visual layer — layout, colour, typography and controls.

Creva Solutions designs interfaces that start with real user needs and end with clean, consistent screens your developers can build accurately. Good design reduces confusion, support requests and drop-offs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about ui/ux design at Creva Solutions.

What is UI/UX design?

UI/UX design is the practice of designing how a digital product looks (UI, the user interface) and how it works for users (UX, the user experience). Good UI/UX makes websites and apps clear, usable and pleasant.

What is the difference between UI and UX?

UX focuses on the overall journey and how easily users achieve their goals. UI focuses on the visual elements users interact with, such as layouts, buttons, colours and typography. Both work together.

Can you redesign my existing website or app?

Yes. We review what you have, keep what works and improve structure, visuals and usability.

Do you deliver files developers can use?

Yes. We deliver organised design files, components and specifications, and can also build the design ourselves through our web and app development services.

Do you create prototypes?

Yes. We build interactive prototypes so you and your users can try the flow before development begins.

Can you design for both website and mobile app?

Yes. We design responsive web interfaces and mobile app screens using one consistent visual language.

How long does a UI/UX project take?

It depends on the number of screens and the amount of research needed. We agree a timeline once the scope is clear.
