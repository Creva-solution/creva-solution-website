About the service

A Logo That Customers Remember

Your logo is the first thing people see on your shop board, bill book, packaging and Instagram page. Creva Solution designs logos from scratch around your business name, what you sell and who your customers are, so the result is unique to you and not a copied template.

Every logo is tested at small and large sizes, in colour and in black and white, so it works just as well on a visiting card as on a 10-foot flex banner. We can include your business name in English, Tamil, or both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about logo design at Creva Solution.

How much does logo design cost in Kallakurichi?

The price depends on the type of logo, the number of concepts and whether you need variations or a full brand kit. Tell us about your business and we will send a fixed quotation before any work begins.

How long does it take to design a logo?

Most logos are ready in 3 to 7 working days, depending on how quickly feedback is given and how many revision rounds are needed.

Can you design a logo with Tamil text?

Yes. We can include your business name in Tamil, English or both, and make sure the lettering looks balanced and readable.

What files will I receive?

You receive vector files (AI, SVG and PDF) for printing and signboards, and PNG and JPG files for websites, WhatsApp and social media.

Will my logo be original?

Yes. Every logo is designed from scratch for your business. We do not use templates or stock logos, and you own the final artwork.

Can you redesign my existing logo?

Yes. We can modernise an existing logo while keeping the colours or shapes your customers already recognise.

Do you design logos for clients outside Kallakurichi?

Yes. The whole process can be done over WhatsApp, phone, email or video call, so we work with clients anywhere in Tamil Nadu and India.
