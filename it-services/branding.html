Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about branding at Creva Solutions.

What is included in branding services?

Typically a logo, colour palette, typography, brand guidelines and supporting designs such as social media templates or stationery, depending on the package you need.

What files will I receive for my logo?

You receive vector and high-resolution image formats suitable for web, social media and print.

Can you refresh an existing logo instead of starting over?

Yes. We can modernise your current identity while preserving recognisable elements.

Do you design social media graphics and print materials?

Yes. We create templates and artwork for social media, business cards, brochures and signage.

How many logo concepts will I see?

We present initial directions for feedback and refine the chosen one through revisions. The exact number is agreed in the quotation.

Will I receive editable logo files?

Yes. You receive vector and high-resolution formats suitable for web, social media and print.
