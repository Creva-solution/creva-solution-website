About the service

Web Development Built Around Your Business Goals

A website is often the first place a customer forms an opinion about your business. Creva Solutions designs and builds business websites, e-commerce stores and custom web applications that load quickly, work on every screen size and make it easy for visitors to contact you or buy.

We start from what the site needs to achieve — more enquiries, online orders, bookings or simply a credible presence — and build only what supports that goal. You get clean code, sensible content structure and a site your team can actually manage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about web development at Creva Solutions.

What web development services does Creva Solutions provide?

Creva Solutions builds business websites, e-commerce stores, custom web applications, CMS-driven sites and provides ongoing maintenance and support.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site we build is responsive and tested on common phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes.

Can I update the website content myself?

Yes. If you need to edit content regularly we can integrate a content management system so you can update pages and posts without a developer.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on scope. A small business website usually takes fewer weeks than an e-commerce store or custom web application. We give a timeline after understanding your requirements.

Can you integrate forms and third-party services?

Yes. We connect contact forms, payment gateways, email, maps, analytics and other third-party services through their APIs.

Is the website SEO-friendly?

We build with SEO fundamentals in mind: clean structure, metadata, fast loading and mobile responsiveness. Rankings themselves depend on content and competition.

Can you provide website maintenance after launch?

Yes. We can provide updates, backups, bug fixes and small improvements after launch.
