Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about mobile app development at Creva Solutions.

Does Creva Solutions build both Android and iOS apps?

Yes. We build native Android and iOS apps and cross-platform apps that run on both from a single codebase, depending on what suits your project.

Should I build a native or cross-platform app?

Cross-platform is often faster and more economical for standard business apps. Native can suit apps that need deep hardware access or very specific platform behaviour. We recommend one after understanding your requirements.

Can you help publish the app on the Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We prepare builds, help with listings and screenshots, and support the submission and review process.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We can provide bug fixes, OS-compatibility updates and new features after your app goes live.

How much does a mobile app cost?

Cost depends on features, platforms and integrations. After understanding your requirements we provide a written estimate for a focused first release.

Do you provide app maintenance and updates?

Yes. We can provide bug fixes, compatibility updates for new Android and iOS versions, and new features after launch.
