Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about 3d modeling at Creva Solutions.

What is 3D modeling in construction and architecture?

3D modeling is creating a digital three-dimensional representation of a building or space. It lets you see the design from any angle, with materials and lighting, before it is built.

Do you need my 2D plan before starting a 3D model?

Yes, a plan or set of dimensions is needed to model accurately. If you do not have one, our 2D drafting service can prepare it.

Can I see different colour and material options?

Yes. We can present alternative colours, finishes and façade styles so you can compare them.

What will I receive at the end?

High-resolution rendered images of the agreed views, and walkthrough views if included in the scope.

Can I see more than one design option?

Yes. We can present alternative façades, materials and colour schemes so you can compare them.

How realistic are the renders?

Renders closely represent the design and chosen materials. Real-world lighting and finishes can cause small differences.
