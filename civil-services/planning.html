About the service

Layouts Planned Around Your Site and Your Needs

Good planning is where a successful building starts. Creva Solutions helps homeowners, builders and businesses turn requirements and a plot into a workable layout — considering room sizes, circulation, light, ventilation, privacy and budget.

We prepare concept plans and refine them with you until the layout feels right, then carry the chosen option forward into detailed drawings and 3D views.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about planning / architectural services at Creva Solutions.

What are planning and architectural services?

Planning and architectural services define how a building is laid out and designed — the arrangement of rooms, circulation, openings and overall form — based on your site, needs and budget.

What information do you need to start a house plan?

Plot dimensions and orientation, number of rooms and special requirements, and your budget range. A site sketch or survey copy helps.

Can you plan according to Vastu?

Yes, if you would like Vastu considerations included we factor them into the layout alongside practical needs.

Can the plan be used for approval and construction?

The plan can be developed into drawings suited to construction and approval submissions. Local authority requirements and professional sign-off requirements vary.

Do you offer Vastu-based planning?

Yes, if you would like Vastu considerations included, we balance them with practical design needs.

Can I see more than one layout?

Yes. We can prepare more than one concept for review, then refine the one you choose.
