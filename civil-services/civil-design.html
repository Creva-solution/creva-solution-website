About the service

Coordinated Civil Design From Plan to Construction

Civil design bridges the gap between an approved plan and a building that can be built safely and economically. Creva Solutions supports projects with structural layout drawings, detailing, site and drainage planning, and quantity estimates that help you budget and buy accurately.

We coordinate civil drawings with your architectural plans so contractors receive one consistent set of information to build from.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about civil design services at Creva Solutions.

What civil services does Creva Solutions provide?

Creva Solutions provides 2D drafting, 3D modeling, planning and architectural services, and civil design support such as structural layouts, detailing and quantity estimates.

What is included in civil design services?

Typically structural layout drawings, detailing, site and drainage planning, quantity take-offs and coordinated construction drawings, depending on the scope of your project.

Do you provide structural certification?

Formal structural design certification must come from a licensed structural engineer as required by local regulations. We coordinate drawing and detailing work with the responsible professional for your project.

Can you estimate material quantities?

Yes. We can prepare quantity take-offs and cost estimates from the drawings to help you plan your budget.

Do you provide quantity estimates?

Yes. We can prepare quantity take-offs and cost estimates from the drawings to help you plan your budget.

Can you work with our architect or engineer?

Yes. We coordinate with the responsible architect or engineer so drawings stay consistent and sign-off remains with the licensed professional.
