About the service

Precise Drawings You Can Build and Submit From

2D drafting turns ideas, sketches and measurements into accurate technical drawings. Creva Solutions prepares floor plans, elevations, sections and detailed working drawings that builders, contractors and authorities can read without guesswork.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about 2d drafting at Creva Solutions.

What is 2D drafting?

2D drafting is the process of creating flat, dimensioned technical drawings — such as floor plans, elevations and sections — that describe how a building or structure is arranged and built.

What files will I receive?

You receive print-ready PDFs and, where required, editable CAD files such as DWG.

Can you convert my hand sketch into a CAD drawing?

Yes. We can turn hand sketches, scanned plans and measurements into clean digital drawings.

Do you prepare drawings for approval?

We can prepare drawings in a format suitable for submission. Approval requirements vary by authority and may need review or sign-off from licensed professionals.

How long does drafting take?

It depends on the number of sheets and the quality of the inputs. We give an estimate after reviewing your sketches or measurements.

Can you update my existing drawings?

Yes. We can revise existing CAD files or redraw paper drawings and record as-built changes.
